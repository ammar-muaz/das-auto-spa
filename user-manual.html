Chapter 1: Getting Started
For all users — registration, login, and password reset

1.1 How to Register (New Customers Only)

  1. Go to the Das Auto Spa website and click Get Started or Register.
  2. Fill in your Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  3. Create a password (minimum 6 characters) and confirm it.
  4. Click Create Account.
  5. You will be redirected to a success page. A welcome email will be sent to your inbox.
  6. Click Back to Login and log in with your email and password.
ℹ️ Service Providers and Admins do not register themselves — their accounts are created by the admin.

1.2 How to Login

  1. Go to the login page.
  2. Enter your registered Email and Password.
  3. Click Login.
  4. The system will automatically redirect you to your correct dashboard based on your role.

1.3 Forgot Password

  1. On the login page, click Forgot password?
  2. Enter your registered email address and click Send Reset Link.
  3. Check your inbox for an email from Das Auto Spa with a Reset Password link.
  4. Click the link — you will be taken to a page to set your new password.
  5. Enter your new password, confirm it, and click Reset Password.
  6. A success message will appear and you will be redirected back to the login page automatically.
💡 Check your Spam/Junk folder if the email does not arrive within a few minutes.

Chapter 2: Customer Guide
Booking, managing, and reviewing your car wash service

2.1 Booking a Service (Step-by-Step)

  1. From your dashboard, click Book a Service.
  2. Step 1 — Car Image: Upload a photo of your car. The AI will analyse the dirt level and suggest a suitable package. You may skip this step if preferred.
  3. Step 2 — Select Service: Choose a wash package (e.g. Basic Wash, Premium Wash, Full Detail). The price and duration are shown for each.
  4. Step 3 — Location & Schedule: Enter your address, select your area, choose a date (minimum tomorrow), and pick an available time slot. Greyed-out slots are already booked.
  5. Step 4 — Summary: Review your booking details including service, date, time, location, and total amount.
  6. Step 5 — Payment: Choose your payment method and complete payment.
  7. Upon successful booking, you will see a Confirmation Page with your Booking ID. An email confirmation will be sent to you.

2.2 Payment Methods

  • Cash on Service: Pay the provider directly when the service is completed. Payment status will show as Pending until confirmed by admin.
  • Online Banking (DuitNow): Scan the QR code shown on screen and transfer the exact amount. Then upload your payment receipt (image or PDF). Admin will verify your receipt and confirm your booking.
⚠️ Make sure to upload a clear payment receipt when using Online Banking. Your booking will not be confirmed until the receipt is verified.

2.3 Viewing Booking History

  1. From your dashboard sidebar, click Booking History.
  2. All your bookings are listed with status, date, service, and payment info.
  3. Use the date filter to search for bookings on a specific date.

Booking Statuses:

Confirmed
Booking received, provider not yet assigned
Assigned
A provider has been assigned to your booking
En Route
Provider is on the way to your location
In Progress
Car wash is currently being performed
Completed
Service confirmed complete by admin
Cancelled
Booking has been cancelled

2.4 Rescheduling a Booking

  1. In Booking History, find the booking you want to reschedule.
  2. Click the Reschedule button (only available for Confirmed, Pending, or Assigned bookings).
  3. Select a new date and time slot. Already-booked and past slots are greyed out.
  4. Click Confirm. A success popup will show the old and new date.
  5. You will receive an in-app notification and email confirming the reschedule. Admin is also notified.
💡 You can only reschedule to a date that is at least tomorrow.

2.5 Cancelling a Booking

  1. In Booking History, find the booking and click Cancel.
  2. A confirmation popup will appear. Click Yes, Cancel to proceed.
  3. A success popup confirms the cancellation. If you had paid online, it will show a Refund in Progress notice.
  4. Admin will be notified to process your refund if applicable.
⚠️ Cancellation cannot be undone. Once cancelled, the time slot becomes available for other customers.

2.6 Submitting a Review

  1. After your booking is marked Completed, a Rate this wash button will appear.
  2. Click it, choose a star rating (1–5), and optionally write a comment.
  3. Click Submit Review.
  4. If the admin replies to your review, it will appear below your review in the booking history.

2.7 In-App Notifications

  1. Click the bell icon 🔔 in the top navigation bar to view your notifications.
  2. Unread notifications are highlighted. Click any notification to mark it as read.
  3. Notifications are sent for: booking confirmation, provider assigned, status updates, reschedule, cancellation, refund processed, and payment verification.

Chapter 3: Admin Guide
Managing bookings, users, payments, and operations

3.1 Dashboard Overview

The admin dashboard shows a real-time summary of the system:

  • Total Bookings, Revenue, Active Providers — key metrics at a glance
  • Today's Schedule — all bookings scheduled for today with their status
  • Recent Bookings — the latest bookings across all customers
  • In-App Notifications — alerts for new bookings, reschedules, cancellations, and reviews

3.2 Managing Bookings

  1. Go to Bookings in the sidebar.
  2. View all bookings. Use filters (date, status, search) to find specific bookings.
  3. Update a booking's status using the dropdown — statuses flow from Confirmed → Assigned → En Route → In Progress → Completion Pending → Completed.
  4. To cancel a booking from the admin side, set status to Cancelled. If the customer has paid, the payment status will automatically change to Refund Required.

3.3 Assigning Service Providers

  1. Find the booking and click the Assign Provider dropdown.
  2. Select an available provider from the list.
  3. Click Assign. The booking status changes to Assigned.
  4. Both the customer and the provider receive an automatic email and in-app notification.
💡 Only providers without approved leave on that date will appear in the dropdown.

3.4 Verifying Payments (Online Banking)

  1. Bookings with Pending Verification payment status have a receipt uploaded by the customer.
  2. Click View Receipt to open the uploaded payment proof.
  3. If valid, click Verify Payment. The payment status changes to Paid.
  4. The customer receives an automatic email and in-app notification confirming their payment.

3.5 Processing Refunds

  1. Cancelled bookings with a previous payment will show Refund Required status in purple.
  2. Process the refund to the customer manually via your bank.
  3. Once done, click Mark Refund Processed in the booking.
  4. The system automatically notifies the customer by email and in-app notification that their refund has been processed.

3.6 Managing Users

  1. Go to Manage Users in the sidebar.
  2. Create Service Provider: Click Create Service Provider, fill in their name, email, and phone. Their account is created with a temporary password (Welcome123) — share these credentials with the provider.
  3. Activate/Deactivate: Click the toggle icon next to a user to activate or deactivate their account. Deactivated users cannot log in.
  4. Delete User: Click the trash icon and confirm. This permanently removes the user from the system.

3.7 Managing Packages & Services

  1. Go to Packages in the sidebar.
  2. View all available service packages with pricing and features.
  3. Add new packages, edit existing ones, or deactivate packages that are no longer offered.

3.8 Reviewing Customer Feedback

  1. Go to Reviews in the sidebar.
  2. View all customer reviews with star ratings and comments.
  3. Click Reply on any review to write an admin response. The customer will see your reply in their booking history.

3.9 Managing Leave Requests

  1. Go to Leave in the sidebar.
  2. View all pending leave requests from service providers.
  3. Click Approve or Reject. The provider is notified by email of the decision.
  4. Approved leave dates are automatically blocked when assigning providers to bookings.

Chapter 4: Service Provider Guide
Managing your assigned jobs and leave

4.1 Dashboard Overview

  • Your dashboard shows your assigned jobs for today and upcoming dates.
  • Key stats: total jobs completed, jobs today, and pending jobs.
  • Notifications alert you when a new job is assigned.

4.2 Managing Assigned Jobs

  1. Go to My Jobs in the sidebar.
  2. View all your assigned bookings with customer details, location, and scheduled time.
  3. Use filters to view jobs by date or status.

4.3 Updating Job Status

  1. Find your assigned job and click the status update button.
  2. Update the status as you progress through the job:
En Route
Set this when you leave for the customer's location
In Progress
Set this when you start washing the car
Completion Pending
Set this when job is done, awaiting admin confirmation
Issue / Delayed
Set if there is a problem — admin will be notified
ℹ️ The customer receives an automatic notification every time you update the status.

4.4 Uploading Proof of Completion

  1. When the job is done, set the status to Completion Pending.
  2. Upload a photo of the completed car wash as proof.
  3. Admin will review the proof and mark the job as Completed.

4.5 Applying for Leave

  1. Go to Leave in the sidebar.
  2. Click Apply for Leave.
  3. Select the date, choose a reason, and optionally add notes.
  4. Click Submit. Admin will be notified of your request.
  5. You will receive an email when admin approves or rejects your leave.
⚠️ You cannot apply for leave on dates that already have confirmed jobs assigned to you.
