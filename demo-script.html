Scroll down slowly past the hero.

Registration — /register
Click "Book Your Wash" or "Register" from the home page → navigates to /register.
The registration form asks for full name, email, phone number, and password. Passwords have a show/hide toggle and basic validation — passwords must match and be at least 6 characters.

After submitting, the system creates the account in Supabase Auth and inserts a profile record with the role set to customer.
Registration Success Page — /register/success
A clean confirmation screen with a green checkmark confirms the account was created. The customer clicks Back to Login to proceed.
Login — /login
Navigate to /login and enter the credentials just created.
The login page supports two methods: Google OAuth for one-click sign-in, or standard email and password.
On login, the system checks the user's role from the database. Customers go to the customer dashboard, service providers go to the provider dashboard, admins go to the admin dashboard. All three portals share the same login page — role-based routing happens automatically.
Forgot Password — /forgot-password
Click "Forgot password?" to show this optionally.
If a customer forgets their password, they enter their email here. Supabase sends a reset link directly to their inbox. The page confirms the email was sent and gives tips in case it goes to spam.
Authentication is fully handled by Supabase — secure, no passwords stored in our database. New accounts are always created as customers. Admins and service providers are assigned their roles separately through the admin panel.

Customer Booking Flow
5-step wizard: car → AI → service → location → payment
Step 1 — Car Details (/customer/book)
After login, click "Book Now" from the customer dashboard.
The booking flow starts with entering car details — brand, model, type, plate number, and optionally color. Previously saved cars appear at the top for one-tap auto-fill. There's a Save this car checkbox for future bookings. The system also restores from a draft if the customer left mid-booking.
Demo data: Toyota · Camry · Sedan · WXY 2003 · White
Step 2 — AI Car Analysis (/customer/book/car-image)
This is one of the standout features — AI-powered car analysis. The customer uploads a photo of their car's exterior, and the AI detects the dirt level: Clean, Moderate, or Very Dirty.
The image is sent to our backend, analyzed using Claude AI, and within seconds we get a result. The AI then automatically recommends the most suitable wash package. If the customer doesn't want to use AI, they can skip this step.
Step 3 — Select Service (/customer/book/service)
The service selection page shows all available packages with pricing, duration, and features. The AI-recommended package is marked with a purple Recommended badge. The customer can still pick any package they prefer.
Step 4 — Location & Date (/customer/book/location)
The customer selects their service area from a dropdown — grouped by zone with the travel fee shown upfront. No hidden charges. Saved addresses appear at the top for quick reuse.
Once a date is selected, time slots load with real-time availability. Any slot already booked is greyed out and marked Unavailable. Past time slots are also disabled automatically.
Step 5 — Booking Summary (/customer/book/summary)
The summary page gives a full review before payment — vehicle info, service, location, date, time, and total broken down into service price plus travel fee. Every section has an Edit button that opens an inline modal to make changes without losing progress.
Step 6 — Payment (/customer/book/payment)
There are three payment options:
  • Credit/Debit Card — mock card form with card preview. 2-second processing animation, then booking confirmed with status Paid.
  • Online Banking — DuitNow QR code displayed. Customer scans with any Malaysian banking app, then uploads their receipt (image or PDF). Booking created with status Pending Verification and admin is notified to verify.
  • Cash — booking created immediately with status Pending. Payment collected at time of service.
Step 7 — Confirmation (/customer/book/confirmation)
The confirmation page shows a green checkmark and a full summary — booking reference ID, vehicle, service, date, time slot, address, total, and payment status. The customer also receives an automatic email confirmation to their registered email address.
The entire booking flow auto-saves as a draft, so if the customer closes the browser and comes back, their progress is restored.

Customer Dashboard & History
/customer/dashboard · /customer/history
Customer Dashboard
The customer dashboard greets the user by name, pulled live from their profile. The dark hero banner has a direct Book a Wash button front and center. Below are quick stats — fastest service, number of packages, and average rating.
The Our Services section shows all active packages from the database. Each card shows name, description, duration, and price. Clicking any card goes straight to the booking flow. There's a second call-to-action banner at the bottom keeping the booking action always visible.
Booking History
Click "Booking History" in the sidebar → /customer/history.
The booking history page shows all the customer's bookings — past and upcoming — sorted newest to oldest. Each card shows: service name, color-coded status badge, booking ID, date and time slot, service address, vehicle, and payment status.
Status badge colors: Grey = Confirmed/Assigned · Purple = En Route · Yellow = In Progress · Teal = Completion Pending · Green = Completed · Red = Issue or Cancelled.
Reschedule
For bookings that are Pending, Confirmed, or Assigned, the customer sees a Reschedule button. It opens a modal to pick a new date — minimum one day ahead — and a new time slot. The booking updates immediately.
Cancel
The Cancel button shows a confirmation modal before cancelling — this prevents accidental cancellations. The action cannot be undone.
Rating & Review
Once a booking is marked Completed, a Rate this wash button appears. The customer taps stars to rate 1–5 and optionally leaves a written comment. When submitted, both the admin and the assigned provider receive a notification and email.
The button is replaced with a green Reviewed badge. If the admin replies to the review, the reply appears directly below the badge — keeping the conversation in one place.

Admin Portal
Login as admin → auto-routed to /admin/dashboard
Admin Dashboard
The admin portal is a completely separate experience. Five live stats: Total Bookings, Today's Bookings, Pending, Unassigned Today, and Total Revenue from completed bookings.
If there are unassigned bookings today, the Unassigned stat card turns orange and an alert banner appears with an Assign Now shortcut. The Provider Availability strip shows each provider's status — green dot if available, amber dot if on approved leave — with their job count for today.
Today's Bookings are sorted by time slot. Unassigned bookings have an orange left border so they stand out. Below that, Recent Bookings shows the 8 most recent across all time.
Bookings Management — /admin/bookings
The main operational hub. Two tabs: Today and All Bookings, with search, status filter, date filter, and provider filter.
Assigning a provider: Select from the dropdown — the booking status changes to Assigned, and both the customer and provider receive an in-app notification and email automatically.
Online banking verification: Bookings paid via online banking show Pending Verification. The admin can click View Receipt to see the uploaded transfer receipt, then Verify Payment to approve it. The customer is immediately notified via in-app and email.
User Management — /admin/users
Shows every registered account — customers, providers, and admins — with name, email, phone, role badge, and booking count. The admin can create new service provider accounts directly, generating a temporary password shown once. Accounts can be deactivated or deleted.
Package Management — /admin/packages
Manage service packages that appear on the landing page and in the booking flow. Changes reflect immediately across the system. The admin can add, edit, toggle active/inactive, or delete packages.
Reviews — /admin/reviews
All customer reviews with star rating, comment, customer name, service, and assigned provider. Filterable by rating or provider. The admin can reply to any review — the reply appears on the customer's booking history page.
Leave Management — /admin/leave
All provider leave requests organized into Pending, Approved, and Rejected tabs. Leave type is color-coded: Medical = red, Emergency = orange, Annual = blue, Personal = purple. The admin approves or rejects with one click. Rejections can include an explanatory note. The provider receives an email and in-app notification immediately.
Reports — /admin/reports
Business-level overview with a Booking Status Breakdown pie chart and a Monthly Revenue bar chart. Key metrics include total revenue, completed jobs, average booking value, and overall average star rating. Filterable by date range and service type. Exportable as CSV or PDF.

Service Provider Portal
Login as provider → auto-routed to /provider/dashboard
Provider Dashboard
Service providers have their own dedicated portal. The dashboard shows four personal stats: Today's Jobs, In Progress, Total Completed, and Average Rating from customer reviews.
Two tabs: Today's Jobs (only bookings scheduled today that still need action) and Active Jobs (all assigned bookings not yet completed). Each entry shows the service, time slot, vehicle details, and the address as a clickable Google Maps link.
Job Detail Page — /provider/jobs/[id]
Click the eye icon on any job to open its detail page.
Shows all job information: customer name, vehicle, service package, amount, full service address with Open in Google Maps link, scheduled date, and time slot. If the customer used AI analysis, the detected dirt level is shown as a tag on the package card.
Progressing Job Status
Status moves in a locked sequence: Assigned → En Route → In Progress → Completion Pending → Completed. Status buttons are time-locked — the provider cannot start before the scheduled time slot. If they try early, the button is greyed out with a message like "Actions available from 10:00 AM".
Clicking Start Journey (En Route) updates the status and sends the customer a real-time notification. Clicking Begin Detailing (In Progress) notifies them again.
Completing a Job
When done, the provider uploads a proof of completion photo — taken directly from their phone camera. The image is automatically compressed and uploaded to storage. Clicking Complete & Submit Verification sends the proof to the admin and sets status to Completion Pending. The admin receives both an in-app notification and email to review. A success modal confirms the submission.
If the customer has already left a review, the star rating and comment appear at the bottom of the job detail page — direct feedback on their work.
Leave Management — /provider/leave
Providers submit leave requests from this page. Three count cards: Pending, Approved, Rejected. Clicking Request Leave opens a modal — pick a date (minimum tomorrow), leave type: Medical, Emergency, Annual, Personal, or Other, and an optional note.
After submitting, the admin receives an in-app notification and email immediately. When the admin decides, the provider gets an email and in-app notification with the outcome. If rejected with a note, the admin's note shows directly on the request card.

Closing Summary
Wrap-up for the full demo

That covers the full Das Auto Spa system.

We have three separate portals — customer, admin, and service provider — all powered by a single database with real-time data across every view.

Customers can book in minutes, track their wash from start to finish, reschedule, and leave a review. Admins have full operational control — bookings, payments, providers, packages, users, leave, and reports. Providers get a clean mobile-friendly interface to manage their daily jobs and submit completion proof on-site.

Every key event in the system triggers automatic in-app and email notifications — so all three parties are always kept in the loop without anyone having to chase for updates.

The standout features are the AI car analysis that recommends a service based on a photo, three payment options including online banking with receipt verification, and the live availability system that prevents double-booking of time slots.

The whole system is built on Next.js 16 with Supabase as the backend — scalable, secure, and production-ready.
