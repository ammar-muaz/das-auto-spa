SECTION 4 — USER ROLES OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  CUSTOMER
  - Register and log in
  - Book a door-to-door car wash service
  - Pay via cash or online banking (DuitNow QR) //if want to test only, just choose cash.
  - View booking history, reschedule, and cancel bookings
  - Receive email and in-app notifications
  - Submit reviews after service completion

  ADMIN
  - View dashboard with booking and revenue statistics
  - Manage and assign bookings to service providers
  - Verify online banking payment receipts
  - Process refunds for cancelled bookings
  - Manage users (customers and providers)
  - Manage service packages
  - Reply to customer reviews
  - Approve or reject leave requests

  SERVICE PROVIDER
  - View assigned jobs
  - Update job status in real time (En Route, In Progress, etc.)
  - Upload proof of completion photo
  - Apply for leave
