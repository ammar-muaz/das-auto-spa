Final Year Project Presentation

Das Auto Spa

Door-to-Door Car Wash Booking System

Next.js · Supabase · Nodemailer

⏱ 5–10 Minute Presentation Script

Presentation Flow

  1. Opening & System Overview (~1 min)
  2. Registration & Login (~1 min)
  3. Customer — Booking Flow (~2 min)
  4. Customer — Dashboard & History (~1 min)
  5. Admin Portal (~2 min)
  6. Service Provider Portal (~1 min)
  7. Closing (~30 sec)
💡 Open the app at localhost:3000 before presenting. Have two browser tabs ready — one logged in as customer, one as admin.
Section 01
Opening & System Overview
~1 min
Say this

"This is Das Auto Spa — a door-to-door car wash booking system. Instead of going to a car wash, the service comes to you. Customers can book online, pay online, and track their service in real time."

Say this

"The system has three types of users — customers who book, service providers who carry out the jobs, and admins who manage everything. I'll walk you through each one."

Action

Show the home page at localhost:3000. Scroll slowly so they can see the landing page design.

Section 02
Registration & Login
~1 min
Action

Click Get Started or navigate to /register.

Say this

"New users register with their name, email, phone, and a password. The moment they register, a welcome email is automatically sent to their inbox — no manual steps needed."

Action

Go to /login. Log in as a customer using a test account.

Say this

"The system automatically detects the user's role and redirects them to the correct dashboard — customer, admin, or provider."

Section 03
Customer — Booking Flow
~2 min
Action

From the customer dashboard, click Book a Service.

Say this

"Booking is a step-by-step flow. First, the customer uploads a photo of their car — our AI analyses the dirt level and recommends a service package automatically."

Action

Upload a car photo and show the AI dirt level result.

Say this

"Then they choose a service, enter their location and pick a date and time slot. Only available slots are shown — already-booked slots are greyed out automatically."

Action

Complete the service and location steps. Show the time slot picker with greyed-out slots.

Say this

"For payment, the customer can choose cash on service, or online banking where they scan a DuitNow QR code and upload their receipt. The admin then verifies the receipt."

Action

Select Cash and complete the booking. Show the confirmation page.

Say this

"Once booked, both the customer and admin receive an instant email notification and an in-app notification."

Section 04
Customer — Dashboard & History
~1 min
Action

Go to Booking History.

Say this

"Customers can view all their bookings here. They can reschedule upcoming bookings — the system notifies both the customer and admin by email and in-app notification. They can also cancel — if they already paid, the system automatically flags it as Refund Required for the admin."

Action

Show the bell icon — click it to show in-app notifications.

Say this

"Every important action triggers a notification here — bookings, reschedules, cancellations, and refunds."

Section 05
Admin Portal
~2 min
Action

Switch to the admin browser tab. Go to /admin/dashboard.

Say this

"The admin dashboard shows a real-time overview — total bookings, revenue, active providers, and today's schedule at a glance."

Action

Go to Bookings. Find the booking just made by the customer.

Say this

"Admin can assign a service provider to any booking. Once assigned, both the customer and the provider receive an automatic email notification."

Action

Assign a provider. Then go to Manage Users.

Say this

"Admin can manage all users — activate or deactivate accounts, create new service provider accounts, or delete users. All changes are instant."

Optional — show if time allows

Show the payment receipt verification — admin views the uploaded receipt and clicks Verify Payment. Customer gets notified instantly.

Section 06
Service Provider Portal
~1 min
Action

Log in as a service provider or show the provider dashboard.

Say this

"Service providers see only their assigned jobs. They update the status as they work — En Route, In Progress, and when done they upload a proof of completion photo. The customer is notified at every step."

Say this

"Providers can also apply for leave directly in the system. Admin approves or rejects it, and the provider is notified by email."

Section 07
Closing
~30 sec
Say this

"Das Auto Spa is a fully functional, end-to-end booking system. It handles the complete lifecycle — from registration to booking, payment verification, service delivery, and customer reviews — with real-time notifications at every step for all three user roles. Thank you."
